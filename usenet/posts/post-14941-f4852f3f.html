HUMAN SUFFERING 
167.  If the breakdown is sudden, many people
will die, since the world's populations has become
so overblown that it cannot even feed itself any longer
 without advanced technology.  Even if the breakdown
is gradual enough so that reduction of the population 
can occur more through lowering of the birth rate
than through elevation of the death rate, the process
of de-industrialization probably will be very chaotic
and involve much suffering.  It is naive to think it likely that technology can
be phased out in a smoothly 
managed orderly way....
