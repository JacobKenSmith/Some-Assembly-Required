You raise fair points.  I just disagree.

Turchie's affidavit would be fair on this narrow point if it said that both
Kaczynski and the Unabomber used the correct and logical version of the phrase.

The sources of Kaczynski's version ranged from classics like "Ulysses" to the
judges in numerous recent opinions.  In formal writing, one would expect the author
to use it as it appears in literature, and not in common speech (otherwise it would
be a cliche that Kaczynski would prudently avoid).

As to what was redacted below, it would have been the author of the
psycholinguistic comparison, rather than the suspect.  In the early spring, we've
seen that there was a short list of 4 or 5 under 24 hour surveillance.  Then when
they got wind of Kaczynski, they promptly dropped everyone else.
