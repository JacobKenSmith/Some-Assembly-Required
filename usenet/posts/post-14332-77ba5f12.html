Thank you Wheaterj! This is what I have been asking my friends to do, read the
manifesto, and give me their best arguments for or against. If someone can come
up with a true and logical reason why this document lacks merit, I would think
about it. So far, Dr. Kaczynski's work has had all the answers to their
arguments. (I still have questions that he wasn't able to address, based on the
necessity for brevity.) But, this was the most striking, passionate and well
reasoned document I have read in a long time.

I fear that most people feel they don't have time to read it, but do have time
to argue against it. It's kind of like preparing a report by reading the "Cliff
Notes". Of course, that's what our society does best. I prepare lots of reports
for management, and am careful not to put too many words in them, since that
seems to be a distraction for them, but lots of charts and graphs. Who needs,
or understands language anymore, when our society is increasingly graphical?
And who understands a good argument?

Dismissing this document out of hand doesn't qualify as a rebuttal to any of
its points. Adults should use their own logical powers to establish their own
conclusions, and then use argument to discover some truth.

To quote paragraph 110 of the manifesto (section dealing with "Some Principles
of History"), "The principles should be borne constantly in mind, and whenever
one reaches a conclusion that conflicts with them one should carefully
reexamine one's thinking and retain the conclusion only if one has good, solid
reasons for doing so."

I think that this is a good general reminder of how a logical argument should
work.

Thanks again!
