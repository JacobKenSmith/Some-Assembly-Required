Anecdote girl is back.

In one of my travels, I went to Matmata, Tunisia (where Star Wars was filmed -
I saw Luke Skywalker's "house", which was a thrill. -I'm a big Star Wars fan.-
Tatooine is also a place in Tunisia, btw, for Obiwan.)

But, it was sad. 

On the road into "town" there was a sign like the "Hollywood" sign, on a
mountain. 

While there, everything seemed backwards. Cute little kids were earning the
money - come visit my house, for a dinar (equiv is a dollar, and it goes far
there). They would show us their houses and their mothers making rugs on a
loom, and take us around. Meanwhile, the fathers were sitting in the dust,
playing a game with pebbles. That is what I meant by backwards. Before
"Hollywood" came to town, I don't know what it was like, but the tourist
industry surely affected these people.

In the guidebooks, they are given the disparaging name "troglodytes", because
they are sensible about their environment and weather conditions. They
therefore build their homes underground, to escape the searing summer Saharan
heat and the winter cold.

Tunisia has quite an interesting history - I bet most people don't know that
the name Africa was given by the Romans after their conquest of what we call
Tunisia, but its near recent history certainly involved colonization (by the
French).

So, Shadow, good point.

It's not so much the encroachment of "civilization" - but the encroachment of
*one* civilization, whose religion is capitalism. There are many paths that can
be taken - there is a spectrum of possibilities for organizing societies. It's
a shame that the "information age" seems more geared to homogenizing us all,
rather than giving us more insight on the human potential. Given this context,
I fear for the predicted bio-tech age promised in the next century...
