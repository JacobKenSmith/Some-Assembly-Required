In article <4pvfs2$pbp@villa.fc.net>, ricardo@paranoia.com (Ricardo Hector
Gonzales) wrote:

> I think the key to distinguishing between nature and other systems is
> to note that man is a descendant rather than creator of nature.  There
> is no escape from nature because we are from nature and to escape would
> be at odds with nature.  But when other people create systems and trap
> us in them, we are in an artificial system that has nothing to do with
> our needs or origin, but is someone else's attempt to control us.
> 
> He gives a hint about how to deal with nature in the section entitled
> Disruption_of_the_Power_Process_in_Modern_Society:
> 
>    69. It is true that primitive man is powerless against some of the 
>    things that threaten him; disease for example.  But he can accept 
>    the risk of disease stoically.  It is part of the nature of things, 
>    it is no one's fault, unless it is the fault of some imaginary, 
>    impersonal demon.  But threats to the modern individual tend to be 
>    MAN-MADE.  They are not the results of chance but are IMPOSED on 
>    him by other persons whose decisions he, as an individual, is unable 
>    to influence.  Consequently he feels frustrated, humiliated and angry.
> 
> -Ric

That kind of ties in with somthing that I've beleived for a long time.
That order cannot be created. Order creates itself.  But when man comes
along and makes an ATTEMPT at creating order, it creates more problems. In
essence, by attempting to create order (government) you end up with chaos.
In almost every case where government has passed a law, it's an attempt
and getting rid of some "evil."  But from what I can see, it DOESN'T cure
the problem it was intended to, and creates more problems. I'm very
willing to deal with the original problem on an individual level. I'm not
willing to deal with all the crap that government creates. Well, I am,
because I have to, but I wish it didn't have to be. I hate to get all high
and mighty, but I wish more people could see that the world will never be
a perfect place, no matter how much you legislate it. I didn't ask
governement to look out for me, and I don't want them to, I'm well
prepared to take care of myself, are you?
