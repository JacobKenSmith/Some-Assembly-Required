That depends on who this "majority" is. Naturally all of us (even
myself) would not be *happy* to have my food supply yanked & my kids
slaughtered tomorrow. 
Plus I'm sure that you could go into an audience of ignorant imbeciles
(out of the movie Fahrenheit 451, let's say) and say "Hey, those
enviro's are coming to cart away your TV sets!!" and incite mass
hysteria.


Nevertheless a lot of people with even limited intelligence realize that
this planet is headed for big trouble. If we could see into the future &
realize that our overpopulation & exploitation are heading us toward the
brink... many of us would prefer to cut back on our luxuries and
conserve now. People have accepted "inconveniences" and "sacrifices"
already (recycling, vegetarianism, environmental laws). People have
accepted the necessity for change... and our "leaders" and capitalist
Daddies have lagged behind. (You'd think that by now someone would have
marketed alternative energy equipment with the same enthusiasm as they
have marketed the better mega-software package of the month.) I think a
lot of us would accept cultural alternatives that would cut down on our
auto dependence & power consumption... but that would require
cooperation, not our culture's present worship of me-first materialism &
greed.

They would probably be more accepting of the planetary ethos if not for
the competing ethos of the capitalists that says "ME FIRST" and "MORE"
and tells them that "those environmentalists are a bunch of left-wing
wackos who are stealing our rights".
