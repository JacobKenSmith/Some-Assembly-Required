With the heat waves around the south, some of the power companies have become
overburdened. I found it interesting to hear the commentary of one power
company official (on NPR) saying that it was just like any other commodity run
by free market principles and that users would cut back if the prices went up.
Someone pointed out to him that power was no longer considered optional, but
rather a basic need.
