UNASPAMER wrote:
> 
> 145. Imagine a society that subjects people to conditions that make them
> terribly unhappy, then gives them the drugs to take away their unhappiness.

Here's my experience with psychotropic drugs: After I was blamed for a
mishap at work and harassed by my bosses, I went on Paxil for 3 months.
(the 3 months period during which I was put on probation and harassed
mercilessly.) I became numb. I was like "this sucks, but I don't care".
It was like I had a wall between my feelings and myself. It did help me
survive, I'll say that. I also had severe night sweats. After 3 months,
the following things happened: my bosses finally harassed me into
quitting, I ran out of both Paxil and the insurance to buy more... and I
learned in the news of an outcast like myself: Ted Kaczynski.

I went off the drugs, experienced a year of depression, became an
internet junkie, went to school & got a degree in computer graphics, and
wrote a novel which is now being put on my website.
(http://www.geocities.com/SoHo/Lofts/2532)

What it all means is, if something is grinding you down, if at all
possible, rise above it & overthrow it !!!
