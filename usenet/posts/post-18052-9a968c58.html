It's clear you don't know much about Earth First! 
It is not  leftist, unless you use the paranoid J. Edgar Hoover definition
of "leftist" rather than FC's; and it is not an organization! 
EF! is dedicated to "wild nature" and has no political philosophy. 
The first EF!ers were loosely described by Edward Abbey in "The Monkey
Wrench Gang."
Far from being concerned with "over-socializing" people (as FC puts it),
EF! consists of individuals who are largely *un*socialized, who hate large
organizations or group compulsions of any kind, and who therefore can
scarcely keep an office running, let alone organize people in
"collectives." 
 In fact, Kaczynski's wilderness-hermit lifestyle, and FC's advocacy of
individuals acting on their own behalf in *small* groups, is entirely
consistent with typical EF! culture. 
Bombing people (rather than machines), however, is not.
