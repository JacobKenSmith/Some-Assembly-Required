This topic is covered in the Manifesto. It's unfortunate that the
tabloid nature of television PROGRAMING eclipses the message of
the Manifesto and discourages people from making up their own
opinions about it. It could be well argued that the users of
the Internet are the most aware of the dehumanizing and harmful
effects of technology. Many Internet users are addicted to
computers and prefer the meaningless virtual world to the real
world. After a day spent staring at the screen at work, they
do the same thing at home. Meanwhile, life goes right on by
leaving them only wondering what could have been.

To answer your question, FC wrote this in the Manifesto:

 202. It would be hopeless for revolutionaries to try to attack the
 system without using SOME modern technology. If nothing else they
 must use the communications media to spread their message.  But
 they should use modern technology for only ONE purpose: to attack
 the technological system.
 
Long live FC!
