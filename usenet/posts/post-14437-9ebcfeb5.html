Shadow revisted certain very optimistic parts of the manifesto about the role
of revolutionaries, and I say, Hear, hear!

The other reason this group is important is that we must be careful about how
we put forth these ideas to others.

See paragraph 187, "...Facts should never be distorted and intemperate language
should be avoided. This does not mean that no appeal can be made to the
emotions, but in making such appeals care should be taken to avoid
misrepresenting the truth..." , and paragraph 188, "...Cheap, intemperate
propaganda sometimes acheives impressive short-term gains, but it will be more
advantageous in the long run to keep the loyalty of a small number of
intelligently committed people than to arouse the passions of an unthinking,
fickle mob who will change their attitude as soon as someone comes along with a
better propaganda gimmick..."

So, this group is a great place to discuss the issues and to discuss how to
present them to people we know who would be receptive to the ideas if
approached in the right way. And to people who may take their time about coming
around. Kind of like the first time I tried squid. I was told it was calamari
and loved it, then I asked again for the name, and was told squid. At first I
was revulsed, but, I really did like it, and still do.

The media has already put so many labels on Ted, that the right approach needs
to be used with people to get the message across.

The messages in the manifesto are changing my life and the way I think about
myself. I feel hope, where before I felt I lived to convenience people who
didn't care about me. These messages can bring hope to other people too. The
hope of freedom to determine your own life!
