I'm not a zoologist, but I question the accuracy of parallels between humans and 
a carnivore species like lions. For one thing, cat & wolf-type animals have 
litters, in which some offspring are strong and some die right away.
Anyway, I lost track of the point, but right now the problem is TOO MUCH 
population, brought about in part by advances in science such as conquest of 
diseases which used to keep population in check. Of course someone will call me 
heartless & ask how I'd like my kids to die of polio, but when looking at the 
world on a macro=scale one has to ask themselves which is better, to have people 
dying of disease, or to have them die of starvation--after they have deforested 
the local ecology in a desperate attempt to feed themselves, thus ensuring even 
worse conditions for their descendants.

As humans continue to overpopulate, they are throwing this planet ever farther 
out of balance. Perhaps this is why the Manifesto says "If the system breaks down 
the consequences will still be very painful. But the bigger the system grows, the 
more disastrous the consequences of its breakdown will be. [ my example: starving 
people will strip every square inch of land in a desperate attempt to find food.] 
So...it had best break down sooner than later."
