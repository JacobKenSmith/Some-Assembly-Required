	I think Ross is making another point, about the stability of the
post-revolutionary period.  Would the technological system simply emerge
again?  That possibility is conceded in ISAIF paragraphs 165 and 212:

"165."  "...It is impossible to predict what would emerge from such a
time of troubles, but at any rate the human race would be given a new
chance.  The greatest danger is that industrial society would begin to
reconstitute itself within the first few years after the breakdown..."

"212.  Would society EVENTUALLY evelope again toward an
industrial-technological form?  Maybe, but there is no use in worrying
about it, since we can't predict or control events 500 or 1,000 years in
the future.  Those problems must be dealt with by the people who will
live at that time."
