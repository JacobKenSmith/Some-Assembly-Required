Indeed, not buying into the corporate media is not active dissent, but it is a
first step in freeing one's mind. Once you allow yourself to think for
yourself, you are a step closer to the ability to act and give your consent as
you really see fit.

One of the other things those books I mentioned do is to show people how much
less complicated and happier their lives could be if they got off the
recreational shopping bandwagon, and were happy with what they already had,
both materially and in life in general. If enough people took heed to those
ideas, there would be serious changes in our growth oriented industrial
economy.
Furthurmore, these changes could be gradual enough that there would be no
bloodbath.

So, in a way, spreading this message is a small step on the road to action.
