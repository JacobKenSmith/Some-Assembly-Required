And that's a true fact. Let's face it... if some unwashed wino, or
pencil neck geek (Yep, I'm using cliches' for effect here) handed me a
manuscript & said "read this, it's about industrial society & its
future", I'd probably go "yeah yeah," read a page or 2, say "I agree
with you", & brush it off. (BTW, I wrote a term paper on this very
subject in 12th grade.)

but as for the Unabomber: I had read references to him as someone who
waged a "lonely crusade against technology" or "an 18-year
anti-technology bombing campaign". this made him so intriguing I just
HAD to know more, and when he was captured, I immediately went out &
bought every paper I could get my hands on. And of course I had to get
hold of the Manifesto ASAP.

So, the sad truth is: if he had not been some kind of really outrageous
outlaw, the media wouldn't have paid attention, and I may not have paid
attention either!
