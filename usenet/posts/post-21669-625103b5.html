Dear Ted,

I am writing this with the presumption that you are FC, aka the Unabomber.
Of course, you will not be able to confirm or deny this, at least until
the end of your trial.

I agree with many of the arguments you make in your manifesto, but I do not
agree with your methods.

Ted, they say you are a psychopath.  I'm inclined to agree, because of
an especially damning piece of evidence, namely your letter to Dr. David
Gelernter, the author of the book Mirror Worlds, and one of the victims
of your bombs.  You sent a bomb to him because you did not agree with the
ideas which he expressed in his book.

Ted, that is not reasonable behavior.  If you don't agree with someone's
ideas, send them a letter, not a bomb.

(the text of the letter to Gelernter is available at
http://pages.prodigy.com/CA/gvmm68e/letter1.html)

(The point of this letter isn't to analyze your philosophy, but I can't
help pointing out an inconsistency.  In your letter to Gelernter, you
accuse him of elitism for saying that people with a college education
will be able to cope in a computer-based society.  What about the
statement in section 188 of your manifesto?  "On a second level, the
ideology should be propagated in a simplified form that will enable
the unthinking majority to see the conflict of technology vs. nature in
unambiguous terms."  Sounds a hypocritical to me.  How ironic that
you, with a PhD in mathematics, start your letter to Gelernter
"People with advanced degrees aren't as smart as they think they are.")

Your goals may be worthy, but your methods not only are unjustifiable,
they stand no chance of working.  To make mass changes in society
requires mass movements of people, not bombing campaigns, of whatever
size.  Perhaps you were unable to relate to other people in order
to become involved in a mass movement.

Your life has been a waste.  You could have devoted the effort used to
making bombs to writing and persuading people to your point of view.
Your manifesto was flawed, in need of editing and critique.  But because
you chose to shut yourself off from a community of like-minded individuals,
even the potential of your manifesto was unrealized.

You attempted to topple the giant of the spectacle, but instead you
have been incorporated into the spectacle.  Few people paid serious
attention to your manifesto.  Your manifesto helped sell advertisements
in the newspapers.

Ted, I think you have potential to do good in order to begin to make
up for the hurt that you have caused others.  Keep working on your
writing, since that will probably be the area in which you will be
able to interact with other people.

Laurence Roberts
larrybob@io.com
