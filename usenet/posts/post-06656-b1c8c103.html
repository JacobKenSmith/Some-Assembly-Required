A while back I made a post about Stephen Donaldson, the founder of Stop
Prisoner Rape, and described his experience behind bars after being arrested
for protesting the Vietnam War.  Well, the ACLU, left wing activists, and
friends of his have made him into a hero.  Well, I think he was a punk --
and nothing more than that.

I think the heroes were those brave soldiers who fought and died for our
country.  I certainly didn't agree with the war, didn't agree with some of
our tactics, but damn it, those kids didn't want to be there; they risked
their lives for our country.  Yes, the puppet government we supported in
South Vietnam was corrupt, and communist sympathy there was pervasive.  But
look at what happened as soon as we deserted the effort: 8 million South
Vietnamese fled into exile and 200,000 died in the seas, wanting to escape
Communist oppression.

The average age of our troops was 19; these were kids!  At one point 100
were dying a day.  What was Jane Fonda doing?  Sitting on her fat leftist
arse reaping the rewards of capitalism and waving a Viet Cong flag while
they were fighting and dying.  Bitches like her make me sick; I WANT HER TO
FUCKING DIE.  I saw the public broadcasting special "Dear America: Letters
from Vietnam" and was deeply inspired, I felt pride in our country, right or
wrong I admired to valor of the soldiers.  And I wasn't even born yet during
the war.

To think of those cowardly reprobates, being priveleged college kids who
evaded the draft, spitting on our soldiers (many of whom were black and
hispanic) upon their return makes me sick.

Stephen Donaldson was not a hero, he was a punk.  A weak suburban white
punk.
