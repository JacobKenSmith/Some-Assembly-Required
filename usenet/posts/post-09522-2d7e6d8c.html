Hi Everyone,

It's been a bit slow on the group, and I chalk that up to a couple of things.
One of them is surely the approaching holidays and all the busy times that they
bring.

Whatever its origins, I am happy to live in a country that sees fit to set
aside a day for people to count their blessings and give thanks for those
blessings. I think that this is a rather unique holiday - there are quite a few
independence day holidays around the world, for example. (Now, if someone knows
of something in the same spirit, please do tell!)

ISAIF had two interesting paragraphs that relate to this little musing.
Paragraph 15, (look it up if you have to, it's about leftists hating Western
civilization) - the implication being that there are some good things about
Western civilization, and Paragraph 183, (the one about having positive
ideals). 

So, here are some of the things that I am giving thanks for: I'm here and in
good health to enjoy this problem ridden but still lovely world; I work for a
whacko company that hasn't yet caught on to the idea of treating its employees
like slaves; I have made some good friends here and offline too, and have a
patient family to boot (but I try not to hurt them too much:-); and I still
feel that my actions make a difference and that I have some freedom to choose
those actions.

I think that we need to make some changes in the world, but perhaps TK was
right about emphasizing the positives to gain support for desirable goals. I
know that ISAIF stated that "A new kind of society cannot be designed on
paper." (Paragraph 104 - Fourth Principle of History). But, along the lines of
people who dream about winning the lottery, what are you guys grateful for, and
following along with those positives, what are your dreams? Who knows what can
be done...

-Susan
