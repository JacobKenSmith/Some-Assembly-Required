> To: Shadow.........OK, I hear you. But--
> 1. Why would the clones be any easier to "exploit" than any child born

Because such a child has been created by technocrats for the purpose of
exploitation.

> 2. If you believe we should shut off technology, when should we have
> done it? Before we invented the water-closet, or before Jonas Salk
> introduced the polio vaccine, or before we knew how to do by-pass
> surgery, or before we had ATM's, or before we had microwave ovens.
> 
> When? Or is right now the proper time?

YES. RIGHT NOW is the proper time. Because (and here's the short answer)
humanity has reached its "climax state" (as in a climax forest) and
there's no room for further growth. We've depleted the planet's
resources and our own emotional resources as well. It's time to stop
worshipping Science and turn our efforts to a liveable planet.

People shrink at laws forbidding research? Why not? We have laws against
having one's own nuclear arsenal in one's backyard, do we not? why do we
believe that "just because it CAN be done, it WILL be done"? Humanity as
a whole has collectively evolved in its ideas about the divine right of
kings, slavery, human rights, etc. Similarly it's time to stop
worshipping the High Tech Tyrant.
You walk about previous technologies that we now can't imagine living
without (the water closet). Well, I think we are comfortable enough. We
won't miss some future wonder that hasn't been invented. As for medical
advances... by and large, do we not have long enough lives and are we
not well-enough cared-for? How much more human lives are enough? How
much more do humans need to take for themselves (and I'm speaking
primarily of those humans at the top of the economic pyramid, who will
be the chief beneficiaries) at the expense of all other life??
