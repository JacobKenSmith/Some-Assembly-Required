I can think of one organization that fits the Unabomber's psychological
profile of leftist activist groups -- Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD).

The Unabomber argues that leftists join activist groups to satisfy their
personal drive for power, and that this motivates them far more than
any actual concern for the social problems they claim to be so distressed
about.  Even after the activist group has succeeded in achieving its
stated goals, the members of the group want to continue crusading because
they are on a big power trip.

MADD originally started out with what might appear to be admirable goals --
anti-DUI propaganda, tougher laws and sticter enforcement of them, etc.
But that wasn't enough for them.  They then demanded a national 21-year-old
minimum age limit for drinking.  They are currently working to get many 
states to reduce the maximum blood-alcohol limit to 0.8%, and I expect
that they will start a national campaign for this in several years.
And don't think that they will stop there; expect them to later demand 0.5%, 
then 0.25%, then breathalyzers installed in all cars, then maximum 1% alcohol 
content for beer, and on and on and on. 

Their latest crusade is to get Budweiser to discontinue its talking frogs
TV commercial because they are afraid children might see it.

Can anyone think of other activist groups that might meet the Unabomber's
profile of leftism?
