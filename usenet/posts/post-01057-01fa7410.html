"We therefore advocate a revolution against the industrial system.  This
revolution may or may not make use of violence: it may be sudden or it
may be a relatively gradual process spanning a few decades.  We can't
predict any of that.  But we do outline in a very general way the
measures that those who hate the industrial system should take in order
to prepare the way for a revolution against that form of society.  This
is not to be a POLITICAL revolution.  Its object will be to overthrow
not governments but the economic and technological basis of the present
society."
-- The Unabomber Manifesto; Paragraph 4
