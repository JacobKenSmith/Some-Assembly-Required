Well, we are both guilty of only quoting parts of paragraph 7. One part we both

It's not certain to me who TK would approve of as supporters, other than a

contradiction between his description of "leftism" and thoughtfulness.

The other part of paragraph 7 that we both left out was, "Thus, what we mean by

psychology (Also, see paragraphs 227-230.)"

I have never listened to Rush Limbaugh, (and don't plan to either) so you may
have me there. I wouldn't disagree with you on rightists being selfish. We've
seen a bit of that in a series of postings from a stubborn capitalist a few
weeks ago.

I'm not sure why TK didn't get along with his parents. I have a feeling that it
was something more than their politics. Of course, rebelling against their very
public liberal politics might have done something for him, but other reasons
might exist for his ideas on "leftism". It's hard to say. Maybe he will clarify
this question in his next writings, since he did want the opportunity to rebut
any criticisms of his arguments.

I don't disagree that revolutionaries are political. I just wonder if "right"
and "left" are the only labels available for them.
